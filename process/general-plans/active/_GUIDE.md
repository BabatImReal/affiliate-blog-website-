# Active Plans

This folder holds implementation plans that are currently in progress.

## Naming Convention

Use date-stamped names: `[feature]_PLAN_[dd-mm-yy].md`

Examples:
- `blog-reviews-scaffold_PLAN_15-06-26.md`
- `affiliate-redirect_PLAN_22-06-26.md`
- `admin-auth-setup_PLAN_01-07-26.md`

## What Goes Here

- Plans actively being implemented
- Plans approved but not yet started
- Plans waiting for EXECUTE mode confirmation

## What Does Not Go Here

- Completed plans → move to `completed/`
- Deferred or future work → move to `backlog/`
- Research outputs or reports → use `reports/` or `references/`
- Feature-scoped plans with 5+ related artifacts → use `process/features/{feature}/active/`

## Lifecycle

1. Plan created here during PLAN mode
2. Plan executed during EXECUTE mode
3. After completion and verification → move to `completed/`
4. If deferred → move to `backlog/`
